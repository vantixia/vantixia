01

//: 01 THE PROBLEM

A closed finding does not stay closed

A model version update, a new tool, a prompt tweak, a fresh data source: any one of these can reintroduce a finding you closed last month. None of them look like a code change, so none of them trip your usual security review.

That is the gap. Your application ships behind CI, code review and an annual pentest. Your AI layer ships behind a config change somebody made on a Tuesday. The controls holding your application together were never designed for a component whose behaviour shifts when the vendor retrains it.

02

//: 02 THE PROGRAMME

What runs, and how often

QUARTERLY

Full re-test

The complete adversarial suite runs again against the current system, not the one we tested last time. Attack techniques published since the last cycle get added.

ON CHANGE

Regression runs

Swap a model, edit a system prompt, add a tool, connect a data source: tell us and we replay the suite against the change before it reaches your users.

MONTHLY

Attack-surface monitoring

We track what your AI can reach and what has been added to it, so growth in the tool inventory does not quietly outrun the last assessment.

ON DEMAND

Questionnaire support

When an enterprise prospect sends a 200-row security questionnaire, we answer the AI sections from your own test evidence rather than boilerplate.

ALWAYS

Async access

A shared channel with the operators who test your system. Ask before you ship, instead of finding out at the next quarterly.

EVIDENCE

Rolling attestation

An attestation letter refreshed each quarter, stating scope, dates and methodology. It attests to what we tested, not that the system is secure.

03

//: 03 FIT

When this is the right shape, and when it is not

It fits if

  • Your AI features ship more often than quarterly
  • Agents in production can invoke tools that change state
  • Enterprise customers audit you, or are about to
  • You are inside an EU AI Act or ISO/IEC 42001 programme
  • Your retrieval corpus ingests content you do not control

Start with a one-off instead if

  • The AI feature is not in production yet
  • Nothing about the system has changed in six months
  • It has never been tested once. Begin with a single scoped assessment
  • You need one report for one audit and nothing after it
