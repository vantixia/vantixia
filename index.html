How an engagement runs

One battle-tested sequence, from first handshake to hardened perimeter.

Recon & Scoping

We map your digital landscape, architecture and tech stack to pinpoint where attackers would strike first.

Scenario Building

Tailored attack scenarios become the testing blueprint – informed by real threat behaviour, not generic checklists.

Unified Assessment

Automated scanning for breadth, manual exploitation for depth. Every finding is validated by a human expert.

Actionable Report

Every finding is manually validated with a reproducible proof-of-concept before it reaches your report. Clear impact, prioritised fixes, executive summary – a narrative your board and devs both understand.

Retest & Certify

After your fixes, we retest (three free retests) and issue an attestation letter you can share with your stakeholders.
