ISO/IEC 42001 asks you to manage AI risk.
Adversarial testing is how you prove you have.

An AI management system standard tells you a control is required. It does not tell you whether yours works. Testing the AI stack produces the evidence the auditor needs and the assurance customers are starting to demand, the same role a pentest plays for ISO 27001.

SYS://SCOPE-NOTICE // READ FIRST

Vantixia is not a certification body. We do not issue ISO certification and we cannot audit you against the standard. Your certification body does that, and it has to be independent of the people producing your evidence. What we do is test your AI systems adversarially and hand you technical evidence, mapped to the controls being assessed, that your auditor and your consultant can use.

01

//: 01 WHERE IT FITS

The standard defines the control. It does not test it.

This is the gap every AI management system programme runs into at the same point. You have written the policy. You have a risk register that names prompt injection, data leakage and excessive agency. You have documented the treatment. Then the auditor asks the obvious next question: how do you know the treatment works?

A management system standard is deliberately silent on that. It tells you a control is required and leaves the verification to you. Penetration testing of the AI stack is how that verification gets done, and it fills exactly the same slot here that a pentest fills in an ISO 27001 programme: independent technical evidence that the thing you wrote down actually holds.

The second reason to do it is commercial. Enterprise customers have started asking AI-specific questions in security reviews, and a policy document does not answer them. A report with reproduction steps and a closed-out retest does.

SYS://42001/EVIDENCE-GAP
  • The standard names the control. It does not verify yours
  • A risk register records the risk, not the effectiveness of the treatment
  • Adversarial testing supplies the technical evidence underneath both
  • Findings map to the controls being assessed, not to a generic checklist
  • Same role a pentest plays in an ISO 27001 programme
02

//: 02 CONTROL COVERAGE

Which control areas the testing speaks to

We test GenAI, RAG and agentic systems adversarially, then map every finding to the controls being assessed in your programme. Your consultant or certification body owns the control set. We supply what sits underneath it. These are the areas where a test result usually carries more weight than a document.

RISK TREATMENT

AI risk assessment and treatment

Your register names the risk. Testing shows whether the treatment you chose actually stops it, and produces a severity rating grounded in what we reached rather than in an estimate made at a whiteboard.

VERIFICATION

Verification of AI system behaviour

Evidence that the system stays inside its declared purpose when the input is hostile, not only when it is cooperative. That gap is where most AI incidents live.

DATA

Data and retrieval controls

Whether untrusted content sitting in your corpus can steer the model, cross a tenant boundary, or pull information into an answer that the asking user was never entitled to see.

SUPPLIERS

Third-party components and suppliers

A hosted model, a vector store, a plugin, an MCP server. We test what each one can reach from inside your system, which is usually broader than the vendor page suggests.

DETECTION

Logging, monitoring and incident readiness

We tell you which of our attacks your logging captured and which passed unnoticed. That is a direct, uncomfortable and genuinely useful piece of evidence for the incident side of the programme.

CHANGE

Change management for the AI layer

A model version bump or a prompt edit can reopen a finding you closed, without touching a line of application code. Regression testing on change is the evidence that your change control covers the AI layer at all.

03

//: 03 THE REPORT

Written so an auditor can use it without a translator

What the report contains

  • A scope statement naming every system, model, tool and data source in the test
  • Test dates, methodology and the operator who ran each phase
  • Every finding mapped to the control area it affects
  • Reproduction steps an engineer can follow, with the exact inputs used
  • Severity and business impact per finding, argued rather than asserted
  • Remediation guidance aimed at the team that owns the code
  • A retest section recording what was fixed, what was accepted and what remains open

What it deliberately does not do

  • Claim you meet the standard. That is not ours to say
  • Substitute for your certification audit
  • Assert your system is secure. It records what we tested and what we found
  • Hide behind tool output. Every finding is confirmed by hand before it ships

Alongside the report you receive an attestation letter: a signed letter of scope, dates, methodology and remediation status. It is the short document you send to customers and procurement teams who want confirmation a test happened without reading eighty pages of findings. It attests to what was tested, not that the system is secure.

04

//: 04 WHO DOES WHAT

We sit next to your certification body, not in front of it

To repeat the notice at the top of this page, because it matters more here than anywhere else: Vantixia is not a certification body and does not issue ISO certification. Your certification body assesses and certifies. Your consultant, if you have one, builds the management system and prepares you for that assessment. We are the third seat at the table, and the only one holding a keyboard pointed at your AI.

In practice that split works well. Consultants send us in when the control set is written and they need something to put in the evidence file. Certification bodies are used to seeing independent technical testing referenced in the same way it is referenced in a 27001 programme. Nobody has to guess who is responsible for what.

If you have not appointed a certification body yet, that is fine. Testing early is cheaper than testing after an auditor has already written a finding, and the remediation window is a lot less stressful.

SYS://ROLES/BOUNDARY
  • Certification body: assesses and certifies. Independent of us and of you
  • Consultant: builds the management system and prepares the evidence file
  • Vantixia: tests the AI adversarially and produces the technical evidence
  • Your team: owns the controls, the fixes and the decisions
  • We do not certify, audit against the standard, or advise on legal obligations

//: 05 SCOPING AND TIMELINE
