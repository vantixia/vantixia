05

//: 05 INTEL BRIEF

Questions we hear before every engagement

Straight answers, before you even ask. Anything else - use any channel above.

How long does a penetration test take?+

It depends on scope - a typical web application assessment runs one to three weeks including reporting. We confirm the exact timeline during scoping, before any work begins.

What do we need to provide?+

The scope (URLs, IP ranges or app builds), test accounts if you want grey/white box coverage, and a point of contact. We handle everything else, including the rules of engagement.

Will testing disrupt our production systems?+

Testing is non-destructive by default and can be scheduled in agreed windows. Anything potentially disruptive is only executed with your explicit prior approval.

What exactly is in the report?+

An executive summary for leadership plus technical findings with proof-of-concept evidence, severity ratings and step-by-step remediation. After you fix, we retest for free.

Do you sign NDAs?+

Yes - an NDA before sharing scoping details is standard practice for us. Confidentiality is part of the job description.
