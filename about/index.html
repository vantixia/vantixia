About Vantixia

An emerging cybersecurity force from India - seasoned experts in information security and red teaming, with a mission that knows no bounds.

01

//: 01 IDENTITY

We are your shield in cyberspace

Vantixia (formerly known as CyberXield) is driven by a diverse team armed with extensive training, rich experience and exceptional skill in information security and red teaming. Our reputation is built on an unyielding resolve to tackle new challenges head-on, without setting limits on our capabilities.

At the core of Vantixia lies a mission of profound significance: to protect and fortify the global community against the relentless, constantly evolving threats of the digital domain. We equip individuals, organizations and nations with the knowledge, tools and strategies to withstand them - and we invite you to join us in shaping a secure digital future.

Led by Rajnish Kumar. An offensive security specialist who has personally led more than 200 enterprise engagements across four years, holding CEH, CPENT | LPT, eWPTX, eCPPT, eMAPT and CRTP. Microsoft, Adobe and JP Morgan Chase have each credited him in their halls of fame. Every engagement Vantixia runs is scoped, led and reviewed by him.
