//: 04 HOW WE WORK

How we work

Anyone can run a scanner. We do the deep, manual work that separates a checkbox from real assurance.

01

Depth over checklists

We go beyond scanner output and OWASP checklists, studying your architecture to find the business-logic and chained flaws that attackers actually exploit.

02

Hands-on, certified testing

Every test is run by hand by a certified specialist, with automated tooling for breadth. AI-assisted testing is available on request.

03

Built to be fixed

Findings are prioritised by real risk and paired with concrete remediation, so your team fixes what matters first, then we retest, with three free retests.

04

Clear and responsive

You deal directly with the person testing your system, with plain-language updates and a reply usually within one business day.

05

//: 05 YOUR DATA

How we handle your data

  • We test with synthetic or test data wherever possible.
  • Findings and evidence are stored encrypted and shared only with your nominated contacts.
  • Client data is never entered into third-party AI tools without your written consent.
  • Engagement evidence is deleted 90 days after the engagement closes, or as your contract requires.

//: CONTRACTS

Contracts we work under

We work under NDA (yours or ours), a Master Services Agreement, a Statement of Work, and written Rules of Engagement with testing authorisation signed before any testing begins.
