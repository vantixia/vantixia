Privacy Policy

How Vantixia collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Vantixia (“Vantixia”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website at vantixia.in (the “Site”), how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

By using the Site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

01 Information we collect

  • Information you provide - such as your name, email, company and message when you contact us, request an assessment, book a call or subscribe.
  • Information collected automatically - such as IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and referring URLs, gathered through server logs and similar technologies.
  • Engagement information - details you share while scoping or during a security engagement, handled under the confidentiality terms of the relevant agreement.

02 Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Site working, remember preferences and understand aggregate usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function as intended.

03 How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the information or services you request.
  • To scope, deliver and support security engagements.
  • To operate, maintain, secure and improve the Site.
  • To send you information you have requested and, where permitted, relevant updates.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

04 How we share information

  • With trusted service providers who help us operate our Site and business, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Where required by law, regulation, legal process or a governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of Vantixia, our clients or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

05 Data security

As a security firm, we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06 Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law or contract, after which we delete or anonymise it.

07 Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise a right, contact us using the details below.

08 Third-party links

The Site may link to third-party websites and services that we do not control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to them; please review their own privacy policies.

09 Children’s privacy

The Site and our services are intended for professionals and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the latest revision, and any material changes will be posted on this page.

11 Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at rajnish@cyberxield.in.
