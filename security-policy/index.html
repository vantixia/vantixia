Security Policy

How to report a vulnerability in our website, and what happens after you do.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Vantixia welcomes reports from security researchers who find a vulnerability in our website or systems. This page explains what is in scope, how to report it, and what you can expect from us. It is also referenced from our security.txt file.

01 No bug bounty

Vantixia does not run a bug bounty programme and does not pay for reports. We still read every report, and we are grateful for the time researchers put into helping us stay secure.

02 Scope

In scope:

  • The website at vantixia.in and the pages and files it serves.

Out of scope:

  • Third-party services the website uses, such as our booking, email, analytics, hosting and CDN providers. Please report those issues to the provider directly.
  • Our social media accounts.
  • Systems belonging to our clients. Never test a client system on the basis of this policy.
  • Denial-of-service, spam, social engineering of any person, and physical attacks.
  • Reports without a demonstrated security impact, such as unvalidated scanner output, missing best-practice headers on their own, or self-XSS.

03 How to report

Email info@vantixia.in with “Security report” in the subject line. Please include:

  • The affected URL or component.
  • A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
  • Step-by-step instructions to reproduce it, with a proof of concept where possible.
  • How we can contact you for follow-up questions.

04 Rules for testing

  • Use only the minimum access needed to demonstrate the issue. Do not view, change or delete data that is not yours.
  • If you encounter personal data, stop, and include that fact in your report.
  • Do not run high-volume automated scans or anything that degrades the service for other visitors.
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly, and coordinate the disclosure with us.

05 What to expect

  • We will acknowledge your report within 2 business days.
  • We will tell you whether we can reproduce the issue and keep you informed while we fix it.
  • We will let you know when the issue is resolved so disclosure can be coordinated.

06 Safe harbour

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will consider your research authorised, we will not pursue or support legal action against you for it, and if a third party takes action against you for activity that followed this policy, we will make it known that you acted in compliance with it.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask us first at info@vantixia.in.
